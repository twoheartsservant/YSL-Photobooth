YSL VALENCIA OFFLINE PHOTOBOOTH

This is the existing Flask-based local photobooth project. It retains the operator
dashboard, guest camera, dual-screen previews, exactly-three-shot sequence,
preparation-frame recording, photo strip artwork, branded animated GIF, QR gallery,
local downloads, and session JSON storage.

CURRENT WORKFLOW
- After the third successfully saved photo, SESSION COMPLETE appears immediately.
- Photo strip, GIF, and QR processing runs in a background worker; no timed redirect
  to session_gallery.html occurs.
- The guest can preview the three originals, strip and GIF, scan the private QR,
  download all originals as a ZIP, download the GIF, or download the photo strip.
- The local operator can review and edit sessions in Ready Gallery and Finished
  Photos. Replacements and deletions update the actual files and session manifest.

GUEST PRIVACY
- QR URLs use unpredictable per-session bearer tokens, not session IDs.
- Tokens expire after 30 days. Each photo, strip, GIF, and ZIP request validates
  the token against the stored session manifest.
- Session-ID-based media routes, archive pages, operator APIs and destructive
  controls are restricted to localhost. Guest QR pages are read-only.
- Phone upload has a separate session-scoped token which is invalidated when the
  live session ends or is reset.

BRANDING & DESIGN
- assets/Logo.png is the official transparent logo and is kept proportional.
- GIF frames include the logo at top-left and Facebook/Instagram identities at
  bottom-center, without a banner.
- Mirror Camera is applied once to preview and capture output.
- The website uses pastel blue (#A7D8F0 / #D9EFF9), pastel yellow
  (#FFF1A8 / #FFF9D9), white, and dark neutral text (#34495E).

Run start.bat on Windows, or install requirements.txt and run app.py. Camera access
requires a supported browser and camera permission. Phone QR upload requires both
devices on the same Wi-Fi network.
